APEX HORIZON PICTURES LEGAL DEPARTMENT

ACTOR / TALENT RELEASE AGREEMENT

Motion Picture Title: "CHRONICLES OF DUSK"

DOC REF: CC-2025-089X
DATE: October 24, 2025

This Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of October 24, 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Apex Horizon Pictures LLC, having its principal office and production entity (hereinafter referred to as "Producer"), and Elena Rostova, residing at 442 Sunset Boulevard, Suite 800, Beverly Hills, CA 90210 (hereinafter referred to as "Talent").

WITNESSETH: WHEREAS, Producer intends to produce, finance, distribute, and exploit a feature-length or episodic motion picture tentatively titled "CHRONICLES OF DUSK" (the "Project"); and WHEREAS, Producer desires to engage or license certain rights, appearances, or assets from Talent, and Talent agrees to provide such services or rights subject to the terms hereof;

1. ENGAGEMENT & GRANT OF RIGHTS. Talent hereby grants to Producer, its successors, licensees, and assigns, the irrevocable, perpetual, exclusive right throughout the Universe in all media now known or hereafter devised, to photograph, record, reproduce, alter, and syndicate Talent’s performance, voice, name, persona, and likeness in connection with the Project and in all advertising, publicity, promotion, and commercial tie-ins thereof.

2. COMPENSATION & REIMBURSEMENT. As full and complete consideration for all rights granted, services rendered, and covenants made herein, Producer shall pay to Talent the total amount of $12,500.00 USD (Pay or Play flat fee), payable in according with standard industry union / production payroll milestones upon successful delivery and signature.

3. CREDIT & BILLING. Subject to Talent's customary performance of all obligations and timely presence on set, Producer shall accord Talent credit on the finished Project as follows: Main Title / On-Screen Single Card 'and starring ELENA ROSTOVA'. All aspects of size, prominence, and placement shall be at Producer’s sole artistic discretion, unless strictly stipulated otherwise herein.

4. WORK-MADE-FOR-HIRE & COPYRIGHT ASSIGNMENT. Talent explicitly acknowledges and agrees that all results and proceeds of Talent’s services hereunder shall constitute a "work-made-for-hire" for Producer under the United States Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 101 et seq.) and corresponding international treaties. Producer is the sole and exclusive author and owner of all copyrights, trademarks, distribution rights, and derivative spin-offs throughout perpetuity.

5. PROMOTION & MERCHANDISING. Producer possesses the unrestricted right to utilize Talent’s biography, still portraits, voice recordings, and behind-the-scenes footage in connection with the worldwide distribution, exhibition, streaming, physical media, advertising, film festivals, and press junkets of the Project.

6. LIMITATION OF REMEDIES (NO INJUNCTION). In the event of any alleged default, breach, or violation of this Agreement by Producer, Talent’s sole and exclusive remedy shall be an action at law for monetary damages actually incurred. In no event shall Talent be entitled to rescind, terminate, enjoin, or restrain the development, production, exhibition, distribution, advertising, or marketing of the Project.

7. STRICT CONFIDENTIALITY & EMBARGO. Talent shall maintain strictly confidential all non-public screenplay details, plot elements, costume designs, photographs, production schedules, and trade secrets. Talent shall not publish, tweet, post, or broadcast any photography, audio, or commentary on social media channels without Producer's prior written authorization.

8. GOVERNING LAW & ARBITRATION. This Agreement shall be governed by, construed, and enforced in accordance with the laws of State of California, County of Los Angeles. Any dispute arising under or in relation to this document shall be submitted to binding arbitration before JAMS or the American Arbitration Association in accordance with entertainment industry arbitration rules.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date written above.
